Problem Discovery

Uncovering the Cost of Tool Fragmentation

Through exploratory interviews with 15+ students and early professionals, a common pattern emerged: users download specialized apps for tasks (Todoist), habits (Streaks), long-term goals (Notion), and note-taking (Apple Notes). Within 2–3 weeks, 73% abandon routine updating because maintaining the tools becomes a job in itself.

  • Context Fragmentation: A habit completed in an app doesn't reflect in weekly goal progress.
  • Decision Paralysis: Staring at 20 unprioritized tasks every morning causes avoidance rather than action.
  • Lack of Adaptive Guidance: Traditional apps punish missed days instead of dynamically recalibrating schedules.
4.8 Average productivity apps installed per user
68% Reported anxiety from overdue backlog notifications
< 21d Average app retention before drop-off
